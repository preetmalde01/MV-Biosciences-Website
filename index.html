About Us

MV BioSciences is the botanical raw materials distribution arm of Malde Ventures, built to give Ayurvedic and herbal manufacturers a dependable, well-documented supply line — without the overhead of managing sourcing directly.

What we do

We supply standardized Ayurvedic extracts and botanical raw materials to two kinds of buyers: end-product manufacturers who formulate nutraceuticals, cosmetics, personal care and herbal medicine, and wholesalers who need a steady, verifiable upstream source. We are starting our supply with the extensive range manufactured by an established, certified Ayurvedic extraction facility, and expanding our own sourcing network from there.

Who we serve

Nutraceutical and herbal formulators, cosmetic and personal-care manufacturers, Ayurvedic medicine producers, and wholesale traders who move botanical raw materials onward to their own customers.

How we work

Every order is placed against a stated standardization spec, with a certificate of analysis provided per batch. We hold relationships with our supply partners directly, so pricing, lead times and minimum order quantities are things we can actually negotiate on your behalf — not just relay.

Malde Ventures

MV BioSciences operates under Malde Ventures, the parent company. Malde Ventures' focus with this venture is building long-term, trust-based supply relationships in the Ayurvedic and natural ingredients space — for manufacturers who need a raw material partner they don't have to double-check.

Herbs are sourced across India at their peak season, verified against the standards of the Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India before they enter the extraction process.

Testing

Every ingredient is screened against microbial and physico-chemical standards in-house, with lab equipment capable of catching irregularities across batches before they reach you.
